# National association emblem sources

All 114 supported national teams use real association or official representative-team emblems. Explicit association emblems take precedence over national flags or coats of arms in the collection, including Switzerland, Türkiye, Vietnam, Poland, Hungary and Indonesia. The Indonesia PSSI emblem comes from the official ASEAN Football Federation association page.

- [JoseArroyave/football-logos](https://github.com/JoseArroyave/football-logos), pinned revision `5d221dfa6849a01dced71c389eb1eafe83ce51e2`. Original SVG artwork is rasterized without cropping to a transparent 139 × 181 canvas and saved as lossless WebP.
- [AFF Indonesia association page](https://www.aseanfootball.org/v3/member-assosiations/indonesia/): PSSI PNG. Transparent surrounding whitespace is trimmed before proportional fitting.
- [lipis/flag-icons](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons), pinned revision `086f7e97d657358203916dbe84f61c2bccaa81eb`: fallback flags, including separate England, Scotland and Wales flags. Each flag preserves its original proportions inside the same transparent canvas. Flag SVGs are distributed under the MIT license in `FLAG-LICENSE.txt`.

`manifest.json` records every team identity, original URL, source hash, output hash and transparent pixel count. Emblems and fallback flags are embedded in the single HTML file for offline use. Existing club emblems are unchanged. Association marks belong to their respective rights holders.
